BridgeBuddy

An AI-Assisted Transition Toolkit

“Empowering International Students in Ireland.”

Reducing confusion, stress, and adjustment challenges through structured, human-centered design[cite: 2].

View Prototype Explore Process

The Challenge: Navigating the Unknown

Relocating to a new country causes immense stress for incoming students. Between securing housing amidst a crisis, managing a strict student budget, and understanding bureaucratic mazes like the PPSN and IRP, the initial months can be overwhelming. BridgeBuddy is a targeted digital initiative designed to reduce that anxiety through accessible guidance and measurable, positive change[cite: 1].

🎯 SDG 3: Good Health & Wellbeing 🎯 SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
POV Define Stage

Point of View Statement

Incoming international students in Ireland need a centralized, trustworthy roadmap to navigate housing, budgeting, and administration because the current fragmented system causes severe financial anxiety and mental burnout before classes even begin[cite: 2].

The Design Thinking Process

Moving from empathy mapping to actionable insights[cite: 2].

1. Empathise

Using digital ethnography, we analyzed online student forums to capture the true voice of the customer[cite: 1, 2]. Common themes included frustration with finding affordable housing, navigating Daft.ie scams, and the stress of PPSN delays[cite: 2].

2. Define

Raw qualitative data was synthesized using AI tools to form our Point of View (POV) statement[cite: 2]. This step provided a focused direction to ensure the project solves the right problem for actual users[cite: 2].

3. Ideate

We transformed the problem into an opportunity using a "How Might We" question: How might we simplify the initial bureaucratic checklist (PPSN, bank account, IRP) so students know exactly what to do the week they land?[cite: 2].

The Solution: Low-Fidelity Prototyping

Focusing on core concepts and user flow without getting bogged down in visual details to rapidly test the administrative roadmap[cite: 2].

Measurable Impact (OKRs)

Objective

Validate the BridgeBuddy concept through rigorous user testing to ensure it reduces transition anxiety and creates a measurable, positive change[cite: 1, 2].

Key Results

  • KR 1: Conduct empathy research leveraging digital ethnography to map core pain points[cite: 1, 2].
  • KR 2: Test the low-fidelity prototype asynchronously with 5 users[cite: 1, 2].
  • KR 3: Achieve an average "ease of finding information" rating of 4 out of 5[cite: 1, 2].
