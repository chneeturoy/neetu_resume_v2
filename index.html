The Challenge: Navigating the Unknown

Relocating to a new country causes immense stress for incoming students. Between securing housing amidst a crisis, managing a strict student budget, and understanding bureaucratic mazes like the PPSN and IRP, the initial months can be overwhelming. BridgeBuddy is a targeted 100-hour digital initiative designed to reduce that anxiety through accessible guidance and measurable, positive change.

POV Define Stage

Point of View Statement

Incoming international students in Ireland need a centralized, trustworthy roadmap to navigate housing, budgeting, and administration because the current fragmented system causes severe financial anxiety and mental burnout before classes even begin.

The Design Thinking Process

Moving from empathy mapping to actionable insights using digital ethnography.

1. Empathise (The Grid)

Leveraged digital ethnography across Reddit (r/StudyInIreland) and student forums to capture authentic voices without geographical limits.

SAYS: "I'm paying €800 to share a room... the PPSN registration is a maze."
THINKS: "I am overwhelmed by the administration before classes even start."
DOES: Spends hours scouring forums; arrives early to untangle bureaucracy.
FEELS: Financially strained, isolated, and mentally exhausted.

2. Ideate (HMW & SCAMPER)

Synthesized raw data using AI analysis to generate focused "How Might We" prompts and applied the SCAMPER technique to streamline solutions.

HMW: How might we simplify the initial bureaucratic checklist (PPSN, bank account, IRP) so students know exactly what to do the week they land?

Result: We opted for a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) focusing strictly on an interactive roadmap to prevent feature bloat.

The Solution: Low-Fidelity Prototyping

Designed focusing on core concepts and user flow without getting bogged down in visual details to rapidly test the administrative roadmap logic.

Measurable Impact & Testing

Core Objective

Validate the BridgeBuddy concept through rigorous asynchronous user testing to ensure it demonstrably reduces transition anxiety and creates a measurable, positive change.
